01 Executive Summary

Existing state-of-the-art approaches combine Sentinel-2 optical bands (10m resolution) with Sentinel-1 SAR backscatter to bypass cloud occlusion. When combined with meteorological variables (ERA5-Land reanalysis), Temporal Fusion Transformers achieve an average R² of 0.82 to 0.87 for corn and soybean crop yield forecasting.

Core Agent Finding: Over 70% of open-source repositories suffer from severe temporal data leakage due to non-contiguous train/test geographic splits. Implementing spatial leave-one-region-out cross validation drops claimed performance by ~14%, highlighting an immediate opportunity for rigorous modeling.
